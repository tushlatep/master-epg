# Strict EPG V2

- Match verified station or service identity, territory, regional edition and time-shift feed. Never substitute parent networks, affiliates, sister channels or league schedules.
- Source fallback is allowed only for the same verified identity. Resolution suffixes may be ignored only for verified simulcasts.
- Logo mappings are candidate evidence only; generic and parent-brand mappings must not become EPG mappings.
- Ambiguous identities remain unmapped. Numbered sports events require verified event-to-channel assignments. No fabricated schedules.
- Measure current and future programme coverage, gaps, overlaps, time zones, and unmapped channels separately. Keep provider-specific 24/7 and replay channels separate.
- Keep full playlist inventory and credentials private. Verify redistribution rights before publishing upstream schedules.
- Run V2 in isolation. Do not publish to production until baseline comparisons and regression checks pass.
